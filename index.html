About Us

Precision

VIVIKSHA DESIGN STUDIO was built on a single belief: great design is felt before it is seen. It doesn't announce itself — it simply makes life more beautiful, more intentional, more you.

We are a boutique architecture and interior design practice based in Bengaluru, working across private residences, premium hospitality, and considered commercial environments. Every project begins with deep listening — and ends with a space that feels unmistakably right.
